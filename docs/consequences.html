Short Term Consequences

In the short term an extinction of the Northern Hairy Nosed Wombat would be a disaster for Australia's identity on the world stage

It would be a symptom of either a major climate diaster and or a failure of conservation efforts

On top of that an extinction would greatly undermine the view on other conservation efforts

Long Term Consequences

In the long term an extinction of the Northern Hariy Nosed Wombat would be a permanent loss of species diversity

This would dirsupt the ecosystems the wombats are a part of and diminish biodiversity this could have major knock on effects on our ecosytems

For example Northern Hairy Nosed Wombats are ecological engineers, constructing major burrow networks that other species of reptiles, insects and small mammals depend on

Biodiversity is not a buzzword it has major implications on the resiliance of ecosystems to climate change

If the Wombats are lost young Australians would have to inherent an unhealthy and unbalanced ecology

They would have to deal with loss of the largest burrwoing mammal, and be forced to confront Australia's record for the worst mammal extinction rate in the world

A permanent loss of cultural and national identity

Do not let it happen!
